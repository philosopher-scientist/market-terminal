Alarms & major swings

Monitor saved markets even while viewing another chart. Checks run every 60 seconds while this page is open; browser throttling, sleep, offline periods and delayed sources can postpone or miss alerts. No monitoring after closing this page.

A level already met can trigger on the first valid check. Repeating alarms rearm after the condition clears, with at least 30 minutes between alarms. Swing comparisons use completed hourly closes near the chosen lookback; session gaps may leave no valid baseline. Maximum 50 rules across 20 markets.

Charts & indicators

The native chart supplies candles, line charts, volume, SMA, up to five EMAs, Bollinger Bands, RSI, MACD, crosshair, zoom/pan, trend lines, horizontal lines, linear/log scales and PNG snapshots. Use Indicator settings to change inputs. Times are UTC.

Indicator inputs, preferences and committed lines are saved in this browser. Lines keep absolute UTC time and price anchors across intervals and reloads, separately for each symbol. A line outside the available history remains saved even when offscreen. Style and indicator toggles preserve the current view; symbol or interval changes reset zoom. Export JSON includes saved lines. Indicators show a dash until enough candles exist for their lookback, which can affect longer weekly indicators on new listings.

Strategy / indicator presets

Indicator settings offers Trend, Turtle, AMZN research, Momentum, Volatility and Custom views. AMZN research applies daily EMA7/32 and MACD20/50/9 from the historical study; its settings preview states the holdout results. It shows indicator curves without executing signal rules or alarms. Choose a preset, then Apply inputs; Esc discards the draft selection. Named presets set daily candles and their indicator mix. Manual period, visibility or interval changes label the view Custom. Turtle shows preceding 20/55-bar breakout channels, 10/20-bar exit levels and Wilder ATR20. The 20-bar channel is shared by System1 entry and System2 exit. ATR on a forming candle is provisional; no trading rules, orders or backtest are executed.

Chart shortcuts & future space

Esc first closes an open dialog, market drawer or expanded view; otherwise it cancels an unfinished trend or undoes the last drawing change. Ctrl/Cmd+Z also undoes drawings, while editing text keeps its normal keyboard undo. Clear lines is undoable. The last 50 drawing changes per symbol survive in-session interval and symbol changes. After reload, Undo removes restored lines one at a time; the previous editing history is not stored.

Future space reserves blank chart slots after the last candle (20% by default). Pan or zoom into that projected region to extend trend lines. It contains no market data or predictions; projected dates use the selected bar duration. The default analysis view is daily EMA 20/50/100/200, SMA 200 available, RSI 14 and MACD 12/26/9 with volume. Bollinger Bands use 20 periods and 2 standard deviations. EMA 1 is available and equals the closing price. These are viewing defaults, not a validated trading strategy or guaranteed trade frequency.

Your market list

Use Manage markets below the watchlist to add exchange-qualified symbols and categories, move markets between categories, or remove markets. Removing a category moves its markets to your chosen destination. At least one market and category remain. Changes are saved in this browser when storage is available; direct-file storage varies by browser. Export JSON for backup and transfer. Import validates the entire list before replacing it. Names are labels; available data adapters and the remote sources determine whether a symbol can be displayed.

Additional markets are chart-only, including added crypto pairs. Only the starter BTC, ETH and BNB markets have surrounding Kraken snapshots. Category placement does not change a market's data source. Charts identify the candle source, refresh cadence and any reported delay. Missing source latency is marked DELAY UNSPECIFIED.

Know your feed

Kraken provides the starter crypto quotes, book and trade snapshots, plus candles. Binance candles use its public market-data API. Crypto candles refresh every 20 seconds; starter quotes every 20s, book/trades every 10s. These are snapshots rather than continuous streams. Kraken returns at most 720 candles; Binance up to 1,000. The latest candle is still forming.

Stock and supported international/FX candles come from Yahoo Finance through the public cors.dev relay, because direct browser requests are not supported by that source. Stock candles refresh every 60s. No API key or local server is required. The relay and data source are external dependencies and can rate-limit or fail. Source delays are unspecified unless the response supplies an exact value. The displayed candle timestamp is the candle opening time, not a last-trade freshness guarantee. General Fusion predecessor bars before 13 July 2026 are excluded.

When a provider is unavailable

Use reload to retry. Privacy extensions, firewalls, network failures, or provider limits can block remote data. A successful download does not establish that the source is current; inspect the candle dates, fetch timestamp and source timing label. Offline use retains the interface, without live charts or quotes; an existing in-tab candle snapshot is explicitly marked stale after a failed refresh.
