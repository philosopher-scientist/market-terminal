# Slow-trend analysis defaults

Research checked November 2, 2026. For the stated aim of only a few trades per year, start with a slow timeframe. There is no universal best indicator configuration, and no strategy backtest was run here.

**Current profile after the four-EMA request (MT-026): daily candles, EMA 20/50/100/200, SMA 200, Bollinger Bands 20 × 2, RSI 14 (30/70 guides), MACD 12/26/9 and volume.** EMA, RSI, MACD and volume are visible initially; SMA and BB remain optional toggles. **Use trend defaults (daily)** restores these inputs, enabled studies and the daily interval. Untouched prior profiles migrate once; explicit custom settings remain saved. Indicators show a dash until enough bars exist.

Daily bars make the longest EMA a roughly annual trend measure for US equities. Weekly remains available, but EMA200 then means 200 weeks, roughly four years. These are viewing defaults; no backtest establishes profitability or a few-trades-per-year outcome. EMA1 remains selectable and equals the closing price, adding no smoothing.

**Prior profiles:** weekly EMA **7/16/30**, then **7/14/32** with SMA9. The user also recalled **1/7/14/32**. These were custom viewing ideas, not established best settings. The current four smoothed EMAs replace those defaults. Periods always mean bars, and unfinished bars can change readings.

The conventional MACD calculation subtracts EMA 26 from EMA 12, then applies EMA 9 to that difference for its signal line. MACD above zero means the faster average exceeds the slower one; MACD above its signal means the difference exceeds its smoothed value. It reuses EMA-derived information. [Fidelity calculation and interpretation](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/macd).

**Closer fit for low turnover:** review a **10-month simple moving average (SMA)** at month-end. Meb Faber's published rule holds an asset above that average and moves to cash below it. His retrospective describes fewer than one round-trip per asset annually in the studied universe. That historical observation does not establish results for these stocks or crypto. A monthly 10-period SMA differs mathematically from a weekly 30-period EMA. [Author-hosted research](https://mebfaber.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/SSRN-id962461.pdf), [author's retrospective](https://mebfaber.com/2017/12/13/episode-86-quantitative-approach-tactical-asset-allocation/).

**Turtle distinction:** the original longer-term System 2 enters on a breach of the preceding **55-day high/low** and exits on the **20-day opposite extreme**. System 1 uses 20/10 with a prior-breakout filter and 55-day failsafe. Original triggers occur intraday; replacing daily lookbacks with weekly bars or requiring closing confirmation changes the rules. The reference to two or three large annual winners concerns profit concentration, not total trades. [Original 2003 rules, mirrored PDF](https://therobusttrader.com/wp-content/uploads/OriginalTurtle-Trading-Rules.pdf), [original rules landing page](https://tradingblox.com/originalturtles/originalturtlerules.htm).

**Forum provenance:** [Goomboo's original journal](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=60501.0) specifies EMA 10/21. A [2013 parameter discussion](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=290961.msg3114748) explores daily 20+6 and 16+4 and warns that parameter performance changes. The recalled 30/16/7 combination remains unverified; it could instead mean custom MACD (fast 16, slow 30, signal 7).

**Implementation update:** replacing the prior embed with native charting removed its input-persistence limitation. EMA uses an SMA seed; RSI uses Wilder smoothing; Bollinger Bands use population standard deviation. Source bar boundaries and finite history can differ from other chart products, so early values need not match theirs exactly. New listings may lack enough weekly bars for longer indicators.

**Selectable presets (MT-027):** Indicator settings now provides Trend, Turtle, Momentum, Volatility and Custom views. Turtle applies daily prior 20/55-bar entry channels, prior 10/20-bar exit levels and ATR 20 with Wilder/N smoothing. The 20-bar level is shared by System 1 entry and System 2 exit. These visual levels exclude the current candle; current ATR includes it and can be provisional. The original document's N recurrence and seed are preserved, with H−L used at the start of finite loaded history when no earlier close exists. This is not a complete Turtle trading simulation. [Original source](https://therobusttrader.com/wp-content/uploads/OriginalTurtle-Trading-Rules.pdf), [implementation verification](../docs/verification.md).

**AMZN research preset (MT-028/029):** daily **EMA 7/32 + MACD 20/50/9**, selected on 2004–2015 training and 2016–2020 validation from 24 combinations. The untouched 2021–October 30, 2026 period matched 15/25 major 15% reversals using 43 warnings: 34.9% timing precision, 28 unmatched warnings and median +10 trading bars from the extreme. Only one matched warning preceded its extreme. This is a separate AMZN-only trend-detection view; it leaves the general Trend defaults intact. The study does not establish early prediction, profits or a few trades per year. The app displays curves without running its research signal rule. [Full report, controls, misses and reproducible evidence](../docs/research.md#ema-and-macd).

**Earlier-warning follow-up (MT-030):** 45 causal RSI14/MACD12/26/9 divergence configurations, including EMA20 context, ATR20 significance and relative-volume20 filters. Training/validation selected MACD divergence + volume ≥1.25× prior-20-bar mean. On exposed 2021–2026 data, it produced five early matches from ten warnings (five unmatched) versus two from 43 (41 unmatched) for the existing preset under the same strict −20..−1-bar window. RSI agreement reduced coverage; the tested EMA20 filter did not change 20-day results. This does not establish unseen-data improvement. No new preset or default was added; frozen prospective evaluation starts November 3, 2026. [Report and full tradeoffs](../docs/research.md#divergence).
